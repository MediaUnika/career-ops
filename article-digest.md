## STAR-ready stories

**Category-defining luxury rebrand (GASTROunika)**
- *Situation:* Michelin-tier caviar supplier needed to stand out in a heritage-heavy luxury category.
- *Task:* Own the full visual identity, private labels, marketing, and e-commerce.
- *Action:* Translated caviar's heritage into an accessible high-luxury visual language end-to-end.
- *Result:* **338% growth**; leading caviar brand across **8 countries** and Scandinavia's top Michelin restaurants.

**Building a creative engine from zero (Acker Asia)**
- *Situation:* The world's #1 wine auction house needed an Asia creative operation.
- *Task:* Hire/lead the team and build brand, content, photography, and the auction UX.
- *Action:* Built Acker Asia from the ground up into the company's largest operation; set a new standard for wine photography; designed the first online + live bidding UX.
- *Result:* Supported **US$100M+** annual sales; **80+ catalogues, 10,000+ images** published.

**Owning a feature end-to-end (Viking Blood)**
- *Situation:* Microbudget period feature with no studio backing.
- *Task:* Direct, produce, finance, and deliver.
- *Action:* Ran budgeting→casting→production→post; raised private/institutional financing and sponsors.
- *Result:* Worldwide theatrical/VOD/DVD; **6M+ viewers** in multiple languages.

**Tech-meets-craft problem solving (Southern Glazer's)**
- *Situation:* Manual, error-prone warehouse wine tracking.
- *Task:* Design a B2B touch-screen system non-technical staff could use.
- *Action:* Designed UX + front-end (QR conditions/images per bottle); led the dev team to ship it.
- *Result:* **+50%** sales-rep efficiency and tracking accuracy.
